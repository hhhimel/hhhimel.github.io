I've spent the last while doing two things that rarely get written down
anywhere public: running variability and path-coefficient analysis on
blackgram genotypes for my thesis, and building web software on the side
(CampusAssist, Gagro) mostly for myself and for SAU.

This site is where both get logged from now on — short entries, dated,
whenever there's something worth writing down. No pressure for it to be
polished; a field notebook isn't a magazine.

A rough idea of what will show up here:

- Notes from the blackgram trials as they happen — not just the final thesis writeup
- Build logs from side projects
- The occasional electronics/DIY detour

More soon.
